# 单臂哑铃划船四步图提示词

Use case: scientific-educational mobile exercise breakdown.

Create a brand-new clean square 2×2 storyboard for a self-supported standing one-arm dumbbell row. Use the same athletic male character and polished fitness illustration style as `artwork/exercises/bent-over-reverse-fly/storyboard.png`: black hair, black glasses, gray tank top, black shorts, white socks, gray training shoes. Keep his identity, outfit, body proportions, viewing angle, lighting, and split stance consistent across all four panels.

The right arm is the working arm and holds exactly one black hex dumbbell. The left foot is forward, the right foot is back, and the empty left hand braces only on the athlete's own left thigh. Show the full body, both shoes, the supporting hand, and the complete dumbbell in every panel.

Panel sequence, left to right and top to bottom:

1. Set stance: stable split stance, left hand firmly braced on left thigh, right arm holding the dumbbell, torso beginning to hinge.
2. Start position: full hip hinge with a neutral back, square hips, relaxed neck, right arm hanging vertically below the shoulder.
3. Peak row: shoulder blade retracted, right elbow close to the torso and driven toward the hip; add a subtle red highlight over the right lat and mid-back plus one clean blue upward motion arrow.
4. Controlled return: dumbbell halfway down along the same path, torso and hips still square; add one clean blue downward motion arrow.

White background, no panel borders, no titles, no labels, no numbers, no captions, no watermark. Clear anatomy, believable joint positions, generous white space, crisp edges, high consistency.

Allowed equipment: one black hex dumbbell only.

Do not include a training bench, chair, box, wall support, yoga mat, resistance band, rack, machine, furniture, floor support, extra dumbbell, or any other prop. Do not place the free hand on anything except the athlete's own left thigh.
